Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 May 2026  |  Last updated: 1 May 2026

Short version: We don't store your photos or voice recordings. They are processed in real-time and discarded. We never sell your data.

1. Who We Are

Run Courage Run ("we", "us") is a fan-made interactive web experience featuring a parody AI character. The app is operated by an independent developer and is not affiliated with Cartoon Network, Warner Bros., or any corporate entity. Contact: cosychiruka@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

2.1 Voice recordings (voice chat feature)
When you use the "Talk to Courage" voice chat button, your microphone audio is captured by your browser, converted to an audio file, and sent over HTTPS to our server. The audio is transcribed using a local speech-to-text model (Faster-Whisper, running on our server — not a third-party cloud) and then discarded. We do not store, analyse, log, or share your voice recordings beyond the duration of the transcription step.

2.2 Photos / selfies (Monster Selfie feature)
When you upload a photo, it is handled entirely within your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. The image is never transmitted to our servers. It is rendered onto a 3D character in your local browser session only. When you choose to share a photo to X/Twitter via the app, the image is sent directly from your browser to the X API. We do not receive or store a copy of that image on our servers.

2.3 AI conversation history
Your text exchanges with the Courage AI are held in your browser's session memory for the duration of your visit. A rolling window of conversation history is sent to our server with each voice turn to provide context to the AI model. This history is not written to any persistent database; it exists in server RAM only for the duration of your active request and is discarded immediately after the response is delivered.

2.4 Anonymised usage data
Our web server logs standard HTTP request data (IP address, user-agent, URL path, timestamp) as required for security and operational monitoring. These logs are retained for a maximum of 14 days and then automatically deleted. They are not linked to personal identities, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

2.5 Data we do NOT collect

  • We have no user accounts, login system, or cookies beyond functional browser session state.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or behavioural profiling tools.
  • We do not collect financial data. The $RCR token operates on the Solana blockchain; any transactions are peer-to-peer and independent of this app.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Voice audio — solely to transcribe your spoken words so the AI can understand and respond.
  • Conversation context — solely to provide coherent multi-turn AI conversation within your session.
  • Server logs — solely for security monitoring and debugging.

We have no other uses. We do not use your data to train AI models, build profiles, or serve advertisements.

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, our legal bases are:

  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — real-time voice transcription and AI response delivery, where the processing is strictly necessary for the service you requested and does not override your fundamental rights.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — for photo sharing to X/Twitter, which only occurs when you explicitly click the share button.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Groq Cloud (AI inference) — conversation text is sent to Groq's API servers for language model inference. Groq's privacy policy is available at groq.com/privacy-policy. We send only the minimum context needed (no biometric or sensitive data).
  • X / Twitter — if you choose to share content to X via this app, your data is processed by X Corp under their privacy policy at twitter.com/en/privacy.
  • Sliplane — our hosting provider. Server infrastructure is located in the EU. Sliplane processes server logs as a data processor under our instructions.

We have no commercial relationship with Cartoon Network or Warner Bros. Discovery.

6. Data Retention

  • Voice audio: deleted immediately after transcription (within seconds).
  • Conversation history: deleted at end of browser session (never persisted to disk).
  • Photos: never received by our servers.
  • Server logs: 14 days maximum, then auto-deleted.

7. International Transfers

When you use the AI chat, text is transferred to Groq's servers which may be located in the United States. Groq operates under standard contractual clauses and applicable US-EU data transfer frameworks. Our own servers are hosted in EU infrastructure.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you are under the applicable minimum age, please do not use this service. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data to us, contact us at cosychiruka@gmail.com and we will promptly delete any such data.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data.
  • Restriction — request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • CCPA (California) — you have the right to know what data is collected and the right to opt out of sale. We do not sell personal data.

Given our minimal data footprint (primarily ephemeral server logs), most requests can be satisfied by simply advising that no persistent personal data exists. To exercise any right, email cosychiruka@gmail.com.

10. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS. Our server infrastructure is isolated and access-restricted. We do not store credentials, payment information, or sensitive personal data. Despite these measures, no internet transmission is 100% secure.

We may update this policy periodically. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect changes. Continued use of the service after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
